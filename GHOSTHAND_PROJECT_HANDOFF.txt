===============================================================================
1. WHAT GHOSTHAND IS
===============================================================================

GhostHand is an Android application intended to recognize the board and available
pieces in Woodoku Blast, calculate safe placements, and then perform those
placements through Android's screen-capture and accessibility/gesture features.

At a high level, it is intended to:
  1) Capture the game screen.
  2) Locate and measure the game board.
  3) Identify occupied cells and the shapes available in the tray.
  4) Find legal placements and evaluate their effect on the board/objectives.
  5) Drag a selected piece to a calculated destination.
  6) Inspect the next captured frame to determine whether the move actually landed.
  7) Stop instead of guessing when the screen, board, gesture, or result is ambiguous.

The goal is dependable, conservative automation on the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 7.
A successful compile or a passing unit-test suite alone does NOT prove that the
game can be played correctly on a real phone.

3. WORK ALREADY IMPLEMENTED
===============================================================================

A. BOARD READING AND GEOMETRY

  - Board occupancy is classified using cell-center RGB/luminance compared with
    a background estimate taken from perimeter samples.
  - The reader records how much supporting evidence exists for the background
    estimate and rejects weak/ambiguous readings rather than trusting them.
  - Occupied cells that cannot be identified semantically remain explicitly
    unclassified. They are not silently treated as ordinary blocks.
  - The geometry pipeline supports fully visible 8x8 and 9x9 square boards.
  - Grid-size selection uses internal cell-seam alignment scoring and rejects
    weak or near-tied candidates rather than guessing.
  - Search ranges were expanded to account for the Galaxy Z Fold cover and
    inner display layouts.
  - Capture-frame freshness is tracked so the agent can avoid repeating work
    against an old frame.
  - Capture/agent shutdown is handled when projection ends or the display
    dimensions change.

B. PIECE AND TRAY RECOGNITION

  - Piece connectivity uses four-direction/edge adjacency. Diagonal corner
    contact alone does not join two cells into one piece.
  - Exact detected piece masks and pickup anchors are retained.
  - Ambiguous or disconnected tray detections are rejected rather than
    silently simplified.
  - The solver validates board dimensions, piece footprints, connectivity,
    placement bounds, and malformed inputs.
  - The tray reader is designed to handle fixed-orientation polyomino masks;
    its complete real-game shape catalogue still needs empirical confirmation.

C. PLACEMENT, DRAGGING, AND POST-MOVE SAFETY

  - Destination coordinates use the actual detected pickup tile as the anchor.
  - Drag calibration is kept separate for each exact piece mask and pickup
    anchor.
  - Destinations outside the captured screen frame are rejected rather than
    clamped to a screen edge.
  - Android gesture cancellation/refusal is treated as a failed gesture.
  - Calibration is learned only after a uniquely identifiable board landing.
  - Post-drop verification checks more than simple occupied/empty cells:
    quantized cell-colour signatures and tray/pickup signatures are also used.
  - If the visual transition is ambiguous, the system pauses instead of
    repeatedly issuing a speculative drag.
  - Completed rows and columns are detected from the same post-placement
    board state.

D. OBJECTIVE OCR AND PROGRESS TRACKING

  - OCR retains token-level and full-line text.
  - Explicit objective words currently recognized in OCR include flowers,
    hearts, butterflies, apples, balloons, bombs, fireworks, gems, crates/boxes,
    and score.
  - Explicit counters such as 2/5 can be parsed when the label and ratio appear
    together in an OCR line.
  - Unlabelled numbers are preserved as diagnostics; their meaning is not
    guessed.
  - Labeled counter changes are tracked by objective type and approximate
    screen position.
  - A progress/regression event is only accepted after the changed value is
    seen in two separate captured frames, reducing one-frame OCR mistakes.
  - A reference mechanic catalogue has been added for certain documented
    objective behaviors. This catalogue is reference information, not proof
    that every mechanic is recognized or simulated live.

E. OBJECTIVE-AWARE SOLVER FOUNDATION

  - A separate evaluator can score hypothetical placements when board cells
    have already been assigned supported object types with high confidence.
  - The optional evaluator includes heart/marked-tile collection on completed
    rows and columns.
  - It models apples surviving a line clear, falling through newly empty cells,
    and being collected when they reach the bottom.
  - Balloon contacts can be reported, but balloon release-count state is not
    modeled well enough for objective-aware scoring.
  - Unsupported object types are rejected; unknown special objects do not get
    invented bonuses.
  - The classification gate currently supports ordinary blocks, hearts, and
    apples, and requires at least 92% semantic classification confidence for
    every occupied cell.
  - Critically, the current live reader assigns occupied cells 0% semantic
    object confidence and marks them unclassified. Therefore, the optional
    objective-aware scoring route is safely gated OFF in live operation.
  - The ordinary occupancy-based solver remains the live fallback.

F. SCRIPT BACKUP AND RESTORE

  - The app has versioned JSON script export through Android's document picker.
  - Restore validates backup schema, IDs, normalized coordinates, loop balance,
    supported settings, duplicate IDs, and backup size before writing.
  - A legacy ADB helper exists at:
        tools/backup-legacy-scripts.ps1
    It extracts private scripts from an older debuggable com.ghosthand install
    using ADB run-as and converts them to the JSON format accepted by the new
    restore flow.
  - The legacy helper requires Android Platform-Tools, an authorized USB
    debugging device, and the old debuggable package to still be installed.
  - IMPORTANT: do not uninstall an older installation or change signing
    identity before preserving any scripts/data you still need. An earlier
    Build139 signing certificate differs from the current stable-key build,
    so an APK signed differently may not update that older installation
    in-place.

G. AUTOMATED TESTS AND BUILD PIPELINE

  - JVM tests cover solver placement/line clearing, piece topology, invalid
    bounds, objective OCR, two-frame objective progress tracking, board colour
    classification, visual state transitions, 8x8/9x9 grid alignment,
    objective confidence gating, and script backup validation.

  - CI uses a cached stable Android debug signing key and increments Android
    versionCode using the workflow run number.

4. WHAT IS STILL NOT COMPLETE
===============================================================================

The following are real remaining requirements, not optional polish:

  1) Restore a clean automated build.
     Correct the known failing simultaneous-clear assertion, rerun the full
     JVM test suite, and confirm that CI reaches APK assembly and artifact
     upload.

  2) Implement actual board-icon classification.
     OCR can read objective words and counters, but it does not identify the
     heart/apple/balloon/etc. object inside each occupied board cell from pixels.
     The app needs reliable icon recognition with confidence values and a
     safe unknown result.

  3) Implement and validate special-object state.
     The solver must understand the real in-game rules for each supported
     special object (for example, balloon release stages, apple falling/support,
     and other objective/obstacle behaviors) before using them to choose moves.
     The reference catalogue alone is not sufficient.

  4) Validate the complete piece catalogue and board geometry against real
     Woodoku Blast screenshots.
     Synthetic tests are helpful but do not replace real screenshots from
     the target game and screen layouts.

  5) Verify placement on the actual Galaxy Z Fold 7.
     Confirm board detection, tray detection, pickup anchors, destination
     coordinates, screen scaling, drag timing, and post-drop verification.
     Test cover and inner display layouts where applicable.

  6) Run controlled end-to-end gameplay sessions.
     Demonstrate that GhostHand consistently chooses legal moves, lands pieces
     in the intended cells, recognizes the resulting board, and does not repeat
     invalid moves or place pieces over occupied cells. Include objective
     stages and failure/ambiguous-screen cases.

  7) Prepare a release candidate.
     After the above pass, produce and identify a fresh APK, verify its
     installation/update behavior, preserve script backups, and document the
     final supported Android/game configurations.

5. HOW FAR FROM COMPLETION?
===============================================================================

The core engineering foundation is in place, but the project is still at the
prototype/integration stage rather than release-ready.

The remaining work is not just one final bug fix. The immediate CI test issue
is small and clearly located, but the larger remaining work is image-based
special-object recognition, objective-state modeling, and real-device
verification of board reading and physical drag placement.

There is no defensible calendar completion date yet. The largest unknown is how
much tuning and validation will be needed once real Woodoku Blast screenshots
and live Galaxy Z Fold 7 captures are exercised against the current pipeline.
A responsible completion claim should only be made after all of these gates
pass:

  [ ] All automated tests pass.
  [ ] CI successfully builds and uploads a fresh APK.
  [ ] Board and tray detection work on real game screenshots.
  [ ] Every supported piece shape has been validated against real samples.
  [ ] Special-object recognition only activates above validated confidence.
  [ ] The solver accounts for the objectives that it claims to support.
  [ ] Drag placements land in the exact intended cells on the Fold 7.
  [ ] Post-move recognition correctly confirms success/failure.
  [ ] Repeated end-to-end sessions complete without unsafe or invalid moves.
  [ ] A release APK and backup/restore instructions are verified.

Do not assign a percentage or promise a delivery date based only on the source
code or synthetic tests. Device verification is a required completion milestone.
